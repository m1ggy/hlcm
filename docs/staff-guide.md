# Staff Guide: Client Workspaces, Tasks & EOD

You work for several client companies from one sign-in: each client has its own workspace, your tasks there feed one end-of-day report, and the client sees the shared part of that work on their weekly summary.

This guide is for our staff and managers (not caregivers). It covers three things that work together:

- **Client workspaces**: one per client company, with its own projects, tasks, people and settings. You get a linked account in each one you're assigned to.
- **Projects and tasks**: where the work is planned and tracked, inside each client workspace and in our own.
- **End-of-day (EOD) report**: one report per day, in our own workspace, that collects your work from every workspace.

The flow in one line: **assigned to a client → work the tasks there → tick them off with what you did → it lands on your EOD → shared lines show on the client's This week page and evening text.**

## Getting into a client workspace

You never need a second password: once an admin assigns you to a client, that workspace appears in the switcher at the top of the sidebar.

**Getting assigned.** Admins (and that client's account manager) assign people from **Client workspaces** in our sidebar, as **Staff** or **Manager**. You get a linked account in the client's workspace automatically.

**Switching.**

1. Click the workspace name at the top of the sidebar.
2. Pick the client. It opens in the same browser, already signed in.
3. To come back, open the switcher again and pick our workspace.

![The workspace switcher open at the top of the sidebar, listing CTK and Sunrise Home Care](/staff-guide/switcher.png)

Most days you won't need to switch at all: **Client work** in our sidebar lets you open, time and finish your client tasks from here (see [All your client tasks in one place](#all-your-client-tasks-in-one-place)).

**What changes inside a client workspace.** The sidebar shows only task pages: This week, Projects, My tasks. None of our licensing, invoicing or payroll pages are there. Each client workspace has its own address; you can bookmark it.

**What the client sees.** The client's people see who from our team is assigned, labeled with our company name, but can't edit or remove you. Each project and task is marked **visible to the client** or **hidden**, and each comment is **Client can see** or **Team only**. Team-only comments never reach the client.

**When an assignment ends,** the workspace drops off your switcher. Everything you did there stays in its history.

| Role there | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| Staff | Work assigned tasks, comment, post updates, time tasks |
| Manager | Everything Staff can, plus create projects, approve client requests, write the daily and weekly notes, change the summary settings |
| Account manager | A Manager who can also assign Staff to that client (from Client workspaces) |

## Projects and tasks

Managers create projects; everyone works the tasks inside them, on a board or a list, and every task moves through the same six statuses.

**Statuses:** To do → In progress → In review → Done, with Blocked and Cancelled for work that's stuck or dropped.

![A client project's task list, grouped by To do, In progress and Done, with due dates, priority and assignees](/staff-guide/project-list.png)

**Adding a task.** On the project page, click **New task**. Give it a title, pick who does it (you're filled in), a due date and priority if it has them, and whether the client can see it, then **Create task**. **Create and add another** keeps the people and due date for the next one. For a quick list, type titles into **Quick add** at the bottom of To do and press Enter; each one starts unassigned, and **Add details** in the confirmation opens it.

**Working a task.** Open it from the project board, the list, **My tasks** (everything assigned to you in that workspace) or **Client work** in our workspace. In its panel you can:

- change its status, assignees and due date
- add subtasks
- comment: pick **Team only** or **Client can see** under the comment box
- **Start timer** to time your work on it
- **Post update** to record progress on work you haven't finished (see the next section)

![A task's panel: status, priority, dates, repeat, assignees, Client can see this task, Post update, Start timer, subtasks, files and comments with Team only or Client can see](/staff-guide/task-panel.png)

**Visible or hidden.** Each project and task is either visible to the client or hidden. Keep internal work (admin, prep, our own follow-ups) hidden.

**Repeating tasks.** Set **Repeat** to every day, week or month. When you mark it done, the next one appears, due one period later, with the same people on it.

**Templates.** In a task's panel, **Save as template** keeps the task and its subtasks for that project. On the project page, **From template** makes a fresh copy.

**Client requests.** Clients can **Request a task**. It waits for a manager:

1. Requests show under **Requests** on the Projects page and in the task's panel.
2. The manager **accepts** it (it becomes a normal task) or **declines** it with a reason the client sees.
3. Until it's accepted, nobody can move it along.

## Ticking tasks off as you go

Tick a task the moment it's done and write one line about it: that line becomes your EOD report, so by 6 pm most of the report is already written.

**When you finish a task:**

1. Tick its checkbox (every task list has one).
2. You're asked **What did you do?** Write one plain line, e.g. "Filed the renewal and emailed the confirmation to Dana."
3. Check the minutes (filled in from today's task timer).
4. Choose whether to **share it with the client**.
5. Click **Mark done**.

![The What did you do? box that opens when you tick a task: your line, optional minutes, Share with the client, then Mark done](/staff-guide/what-did-you-do.png)

**When you made progress but aren't finished:** open the task and use **Post update** in its panel. Same line, minutes and share choice.

**Where the line goes.** It shows on the task (and to the client, when shared) and lands on today's EOD as a line marked *From updates on the task*. More updates on the same task join the same line. If you'd already submitted today's report, the line is still added, marked *added after submitting*.

Subtasks can skip the question. Unticking a finished task reopens it.

**Writing a good line.** The client may read it word for word on their This week page and in the evening text, so write the outcome, not the activity: "Sent the signed lease to the landlord" beats "worked on lease".

## All your client tasks in one place

Start your day on **Client work** in our own sidebar (under **Tasks**): it lists your open tasks in every client workspace you're assigned to, so you don't have to switch into each one to see what's due.

![Client work with a timer running on one client task: the row shows Stop, and the header shows the running timer and Clock out](/staff-guide/client-work.png)

You can do almost all of your client work from this page, without switching workspaces:

- **Time it.** Click **Start** on a task's row; it turns into **Stop** while it runs. The timer also shows in the header with the client's name, and its Stop works too. Starting a timer clocks you in if you aren't already.
- **Open it.** Click a task to open its full panel right here: status, priority, dates, assignees, description, subtasks, comments, **Post update** and **Start timer**.
- **Finish it.** Tick its checkbox and write what you did (previous section).
- **Files** are the one exception: the panel lists them, and **Open in [client] for files** takes you to the client's workspace to download or add one.

Only one timer runs at a time across all your workspaces: starting one anywhere stops the other. Time on a client's task counts in that client's workspace, the same as if you'd switched in.

![A client task's panel opened from Client work, without leaving our workspace](/staff-guide/client-work-panel.png)

Our own internal tasks live under **Projects & tasks**, in the same Tasks group.

| Where you are | Sidebar pages for tasks |
| --- | --- |
| Our workspace | Projects & tasks, Client work, End of day |
| A client workspace | This week, Projects, My tasks |

## The end-of-day (EOD) report

Everyone except caregivers submits one EOD report a day, under **End of day** in our sidebar; it covers your work in our workspace and every client workspace, and you get a reminder at 6 pm if yours isn't in.

**1. Start the day.** Click **Start day** on the End of day page, or the prompt when you clock in. Today's report opens with:

- a line for each of your open tasks, in every workspace (client tasks are marked with the workspace's name)
- a line for anything you timed today, minutes already filled in from the timer

![Today's end-of-day report after Start day: a line per open task with its status, What you did, Minutes and Share with the client](/staff-guide/eod.png)

**2. Fill it as you go.** Lines from ticked tasks and posted updates appear on their own (see [Ticking tasks off as you go](#ticking-tasks-off-as-you-go)). You can also type straight into any line. The report saves itself as you type.

- **Move a task along on its line** (In progress, Completed, Blocked…). The change is applied to the task when you submit, and shows in the task's history, including in client workspaces.
- **Add a task** puts another of your tasks on the report.
- **Work not on a task** covers meetings, calls and the like.
- **Shared or internal.** A line on a client's task can be shared with that client; it then shows on their This week page as an update from the team. An internal note never is.

**3. Finish and submit.** Write a short **summary**, any **blockers**, and your **plan for tomorrow**, then click **Submit report**. Lines you left empty are dropped. Clocking out reminds you if you haven't submitted.

**4. Fixing a submitted report.** You can reopen it until the end of that day. After that, ask a manager.

**For managers: the Team tab.** On the same page, **Team** shows everyone's report for the day (submitted, drafting or missing), a comment thread on each report, who is expected to submit daily, and a spreadsheet export of a day or a week.

## What the client sees

The client's home page is **This week**: Monday to Sunday, what was finished, moved forward or added each day, built from the tasks and shared updates you post. When you're in their workspace, you see exactly what they see.

![A client's This week page: totals for the week and a card per day with what was finished and the day's note](/staff-guide/this-week.png)

**Daily and weekly notes (managers).** Managers can write a short note for any day or week. Claude drafts these notes automatically each evening; edit or rewrite any of them. A note you edit is never overwritten.

**Hours stay hidden** from the client unless a manager turns them on in the page's settings.

**Monday email.** Every Monday morning the client's people get last week's summary by email.

**Evening text.** When a manager turns on **Evening text to the client** in the This week settings, the day's note goes out by text around 8 pm on days with updates.

- Check or edit the day's note before 8 pm.
- To skip a day, untick **Text today's update to the client tonight** in that day's panel.
- Afterwards the panel shows when it went and to how many people.

![Summary settings on This week: Show hours to the client, AI-written notes, Monday email, Evening text to the client](/staff-guide/summary-settings.png)

**Who gets the text.** Only the client's people who are signed up. Either:

- they sign up themselves in their own **Settings** (tick the daily update, add their mobile number), or
- an admin or the client's account manager signs them up from **Client workspaces** in our sidebar → pick the client → the **client's people** card: enter the mobile number, tick **Evening text**, tick the box confirming they agreed, and **Save**.

![Client workspaces page: the client's people card with a mobile number, an Evening text box and the consent box for a new sign-up](/staff-guide/client-people.png)

Only sign someone up after they've agreed to get texts. Anyone can reply STOP; after that, only they can turn texts back on.

**What went out.** Admins and the client's account manager can see every evening text sent to a client under **Client workspaces** → pick the client → **Evening texts sent**: the day, when it went, the text, and each person it went to, marked Sent or Failed.

## Your daily routine

Five habits keep the client's page and your report current with almost no end-of-day writing.

1. **Morning:** clock in, click **Start day**, and open **Client work** to see what's due across all clients.
2. **While working:** click **Start** on the task you're on.
3. **Each finished task:** tick it, write one outcome line, choose shared or internal.
4. **Progress on unfinished work:** **Post update** on the task.
5. **Before 6 pm:** on **End of day**, add the summary, blockers and tomorrow's plan, then **Submit report**.

Managers also: check client **Requests**, and read the day's note on each client's **This week** before the 8 pm text goes out.

## FAQ

**I don't see a client in my switcher.** You aren't assigned to it yet, or the assignment ended. Ask an admin or that client's account manager.

**Can the client see my comment?** Only if you chose **Client can see**. Team-only comments and internal EOD notes never reach them.

**I ticked a task by mistake.** Untick it; the task reopens.

**I forgot to submit yesterday's report.** You can only reopen a report on the same day. Ask a manager.

**My timer stopped on its own.** You started another one somewhere else. Only one timer runs at a time, across our workspace and every client workspace.

**My update shows "added after submitting".** That's fine: you ticked or updated a task after submitting, so the line was added to the submitted report.

**A client isn't getting the evening text.** Check that the evening text is on in their This week settings, that they're signed up with a mobile number (Client workspaces → their people), that the day had updates, and that the day wasn't skipped. If they replied STOP, only they can turn texts back on.
