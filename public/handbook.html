Internal handbook

Running a case in HCLM, start to finish

A quick visual tour of the day-to-day screens, roughly in the order you'd actually use them: set up a project, add your clients to it, open a case for each one, then work that case day to day.

For staff & managers Screens as of August 2026 ~8 minute read
HCLM organizes work in three layers: a Project groups related licensing work, each Client — the facility or business being licensed — belongs to one, and each Application (a case) is a single license or renewal filed for a client. This handbook walks through the screens in that order. For a click-through version inside the app itself, see Getting more help.
01

Signing in

Sign in with the email and password your admin set up for you. If your account has two-factor authentication turned on, you'll be asked for a code right after.

02

Projects

A Project is where work starts — a batch of related licensing work, like a renewal cycle or a group of facilities under one owner. Everything else in HCLM hangs off a project, so it's the first thing you create. Click New Project, give it a name, and it's ready for clients.

03

Clients

A Client is the actual facility or business you're getting licensed — business details, address, and the owner's contact info. Clients are added from inside a project, with New Client, so every client is tied to one from the moment it's created.

Open a client for a running notes thread (use @ to mention someone), every case tied to them, and a full audit log.

04

Your case list

Applications is what HCLM calls a case — one per license or renewal you're working for a client. The list shows every case's client, who owns it, checklist progress, and current status. Use the filter chips to narrow it down, or click New Application to start one — you'll pick which client it's for, plus its license type and case type if you know them yet.

05

Board view

Prefer to see cases grouped by status? Switch to Board and drag a card between columns to change its status — useful for a quick pipeline check without opening anything.

06

Inside a case

Click any case to open it. The Checklist on the left is the actual work — tasks grouped into phases, each with a status, an owner, and a due date. Above the tabbed panel on the right, a Client card shows who you're licensing for — business and owner contact info, with a link to their full profile. The tabs below it cover the case's details, files, generated documents, comments, sharing, and its audit log.

i Drag the grip handle on any task to reorder it, or use the expand icon to open its full detail sheet — covered in step 11.
07

Files & version history

The Files tab holds everything uploaded to the case, shown as cards. Click the info button on any file to open a drawer with download, e-signing (for PDFs), delete, and the complete version history — including reverting to an older version.

i Once a PDF has been signed, its content locks — no new versions can be uploaded over it, so the signed copy always matches what was actually signed.
08

Generating documents

Admins upload a .docx template with {merge_tags} in it under Admin → Document Templates — each tag maps to auto-filled data (client name, license type, today's date, and more) or gets left as a prompt someone fills in later. From a case's Documents tab, pick the template, fill in anything it still needs, and generate. Every file it produces keeps its own status and stays separate from the source template.

09

Audit log

Every status change, file upload, and edit to a case is recorded automatically. The Audit Log tab is a plain-English timeline of it — useful when you need to know exactly when something changed, and who changed it.

10

My Tasks

Not everything is tied to a case. My Tasks is for recurring or one-off work — credential renewals, cleanup jobs, anything ops-related. It has its own list and board view, same as Applications.

11

A task, expanded

Expanding any task — from a case's checklist or from My Tasks — opens its full detail: description, status, assignee, due date, attached files, a comment thread (use @ to mention someone), and an activity panel logging every change made to that specific task.

12

Time & payroll

The clock button in the header works on any page — clock in when you start, out when you're done, and your sessions land on the Time page. Admins and managers also get a company-wide report there: pick a user (or everyone) and a date range for total hours × rate, downloadable as a PDF. Admins can send it straight out as a bank transfer through Wise, once someone's added their own payout details on their Account page — the fields there change automatically by country, pulled live from Wise rather than hardcoded per currency.

i A session still in progress shows up but doesn't count toward a report's total until it's clocked out.
13

Notifications

The bell in the top right tells you when a task is reassigned to you, someone requests your review, a case's status changes, or a case gets shared with you. Click a notification to jump straight to it, or clear everything at once.

14

Quick search

Press Ctrl + K (⌘ + K on a Mac) from anywhere in the app to jump straight to a case, client, or document by name — faster than clicking back to a list.
